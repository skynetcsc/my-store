Job seeker safety checklist

  • Never pay registration, interview, training or security fees to get a job.
  • Verify the employer's name, office address, official email and contact number independently.
  • Do not share OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, internet banking password or original documents with unknown people.
  • Confirm in-hand salary, deductions, timings, weekly off, location and written offer terms before joining.
