About Us

A place where learning
feels simple & supportive

Verma Coaching Center is a neighbourhood coaching institute located near UCO Bank, VPO Ramgarh, District Panchkula. We teach students from Class 5 to Class 12 following the CBSE, HBSE and ICSE syllabi.

Our approach is simple — small batches, clear explanations and regular practice. Every student gets personal attention so that doubts are solved the same day and concepts are built step by step, not by rote learning.
