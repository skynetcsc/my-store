Plan around the season

When should you go?

Weather aur comfort ko dhyan mein rakh kar apni outing plan karein.

🌸

Winter & spring

Nov–Mar: gardens, heritage walks, Rock Garden, Pinjore aur Kasauli trips ke liye comfortable season. Rose Garden ke flowers ke liye Feb–Mar achha time.

☀️

Summer

Apr–Jun: outdoor sightseeing subah jaldi ya late afternoon karein. Morni Hills jaise hill-side day trips ke liye paani aur sun protection saath rakhein.

🌦️

Monsoon

Jul–Sep: greenery achhi hoti hai, lekin Morni/Kasauli ki hill roads par rain forecast check karein. Slippery trails aur heavy-rain travel avoid karein.
